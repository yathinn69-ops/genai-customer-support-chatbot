Company Knowledge Base Policy - Version 3

Employees must follow the latest approved company policies.

All knowledge-base documents are validated before processing.
Duplicate documents are detected automatically.
Approved documents are versioned before activation.

Updates are activated only during the configured maintenance window.
After activation, the service is monitored for health.

If a health check fails, the system automatically rolls back
to the previous active version.

All important processing, activation, and rollback events are
recorded in the audit log for traceability.
